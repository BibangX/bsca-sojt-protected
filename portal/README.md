# BSCA SOJT Student Portal

Public static portal with pirate map, Captain’s Briefing, matching step and company popups, document templates, company references, and Google Forms submission links.

## Publish on GitHub Pages
Extract this ZIP. Upload its contents to your repository root, keeping index.html at the root and preserving the assets and templates folders. Replace matching files. Keep your existing Pages publishing settings.

No installation or build is required. This version has no portal login or email verification. Google Forms manage their own sign-in and response settings separately.

Edit resources.js to maintain submission links and template references. Supplied documents remain unchanged; sample templates require coordinator review before official use.
